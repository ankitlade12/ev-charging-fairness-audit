# AI assistance disclosure

Updated 21 September 2026. The named author manuscript includes an acknowledgment disclosure. The anonymous review copy includes identical non-identifying content under **AI Assistance Disclosure**. This restores in-article disclosure; the previous 19 September draft kept it only in this working document.

> OpenAI Codex (GPT-6) assisted with literature discovery, experimental design, simulator and analysis code, verification, and drafting and revision throughout the manuscript. Reported numerical results come from the supplied experimental artifacts. The human authors retain responsibility for the final work.

This statement does not assert that independent human verification has occurred. The human authors must review the manuscript and artifacts before approving submission. No AI system is listed as an author.

IEEE specifies acknowledgments as the disclosure location, while CCWC instructs anonymous review authors to remove acknowledgments. The separate disclosure heading is a transparent working resolution, not a conference-approved exception. Confirm placement before upload; see [the submission checklist](submission_checklist.md), [IEEE policy](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-policies/) and [CCWC instructions](https://ieee-ccwc.org/submissions/).
