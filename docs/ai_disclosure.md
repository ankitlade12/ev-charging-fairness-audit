# AI assistance disclosure

The 19 September 2026 editorial revision keeps this disclosure outside the local manuscript at the author's request. Before IEEE submission, restore the required disclosure in the location specified by the applicable submission instructions. This separate working document does not replace an in-article disclosure.

Suggested disclosure for human author review:

> OpenAI Codex (GPT-6) assisted with literature discovery, the research assessment, experimental design, implementation of the simulator and analysis scripts, test construction, and drafting and revision of all manuscript sections. Numerical results were produced by running the supplied experiment code; they were not generated as invented measurements. Human authors must review and take responsibility for the code, references, interpretation and final manuscript before submission.

This accurately records assistance; it does not assert that human verification has already occurred. For a final submission, replace the last sentence with a factual description of the verification actually performed. No AI system should be listed as an author. Keep author identities out of the blind review version. Confirm disclosure placement against IEEE and CCWC instructions; see `submission_checklist.md`.
